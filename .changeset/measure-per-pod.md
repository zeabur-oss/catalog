# The index publishes idle usage per workload pod

Each entry of `measured` in `index.json` has `pods` instead of `idle` and `requests`. A row is a workload with its `kind` and the `memoryMiB` and `cpum` of one pod of it, the median over its pods. Totals are not stored. A reader multiplies each row by the replicas of the objects the component renders and reads requests from them too. The measurements are regenerated by the next full run.
